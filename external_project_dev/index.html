Mission

Owners Richard and Linda Barret spent several decades in the trucking industry and know firsthand that long trips take more than just a full tank. They opened Rocket Fuel Coffee Co. with the goal of becoming more than a typical gas station — a place where truckers, commuters, and travelers can fuel their bodies as well as their vehicles. What most stations overlook, they built their business around: good coffee, real service, and a welcoming spot to recharge at any hour. With the lobby and small convenience store open 24 hours a day, you can stop by anytime for a sip, a bite, or a glug and keep your journey moving.

Convenience Store

While coffee can take you to great heights, we know there is always one other thing you need. Something forgotten at home, left at a hotel, or somehow slipped into the great abyss that might be under your car seat. Rocket Fuel Co. has a small convenience store attached, filled with all the travel essentials and a little more. Be sure to check the local business display, where you will find great souvenirs and items to help with day-to-day life.
